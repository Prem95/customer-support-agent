# Claim Settlement
Keywords: settlement, payout, payment, reimbursement, replacement, approved, how long, bank

Once a claim is approved, the customer chooses how it is settled:

- **Cash settlement** - paid to the registered bank account within 5 business days, less the policy excess.
- **Replacement** - an equivalent item sourced through an approved supplier, subject to availability.
- **Repair or reinstatement** - arranged with an approved supplier and paid directly by the insurer.

Settlement is based on the assessed value at the time of loss, capped by the annual and per-item limits on the policy schedule. Where a claim exceeds the remaining annual limit, the balance is the customer's responsibility.

Agents should confirm the excess amount with the customer before settlement is arranged, and check that bank details on file are current for cash settlements.
