# Coverage and Exclusions
Keywords: coverage, covered, cover, exclusion, excluded, limit, excess, benefit

**Covered under a standard policy:**
- Accidental damage to insured items
- Theft and burglary, with a police report filed within 48 hours
- Loss of insured items, subject to the annual limit
- Fire, flood, and storm damage
- Liability for accidental injury or damage to third parties, where the policy includes liability cover

**Not covered:**
- Wear, tear, and gradual deterioration
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect function or value
- Damage caused by unauthorised repair or modification
- Items used commercially unless declared on the policy
- Incidents occurring while the policy is lapsed for non-payment
- Deliberate damage or gross negligence

Each claim is subject to the excess stated on the policy schedule, deducted from the settlement. Annual limits and any per-item limits also appear on the schedule. Agents should confirm the customer's cover level before advising whether an incident is covered.
