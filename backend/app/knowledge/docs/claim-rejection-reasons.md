# Common Claim Rejection Reasons
Keywords: claim, rejected, rejection, denied, refused, declined, why

The most common reasons a claim is rejected:

1. **Incomplete documentation** - missing evidence, receipts, or an incident report.
2. **Late notification** - incident reported more than 30 days after it occurred.
3. **Excluded cause** - the incident falls under a policy exclusion.
4. **Policy lapsed** - premium unpaid at the time of the incident.
5. **Duplicate claim** - a claim for the same incident already exists.
6. **Item not covered** - the item was not listed or declared on the policy.
7. **Value below excess** - the assessed loss is lower than the policy excess.

Every rejection carries a code recorded on the claim, and the rejection letter states the code and the customer's appeal rights. Agents can look the code up with the claim reference number and should explain it plainly before discussing an appeal.
