# How to File a Claim
Keywords: claim, file, submit, new claim, start, report, incident, loss

Customers can file a claim through the customer portal or by asking an agent, within 30 days of the incident.

Required to file:
1. Policy number (format: POL-XXXXXXX)
2. Date and description of the incident
3. Supporting evidence (photographs, invoices, or receipts)
4. Proof of ownership or valuation where the loss exceeds the standard limit
5. Police or incident report reference, where applicable

Once submitted, the customer receives a claim reference number (format: CLM-XXXXXXX). Standard assessment takes 3-5 business days. Agents should collect the required items one at a time and confirm each is complete before submission, since incomplete documentation is the most common cause of rejection.
