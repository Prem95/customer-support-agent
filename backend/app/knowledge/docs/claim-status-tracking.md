# Claim Status Tracking
Keywords: claim, status, track, progress, pending, update, waiting, timeline

Claim statuses and what they mean:

- **Submitted** - received, awaiting assessment (0-1 business days).
- **In Assessment** - assessor reviewing the claim and evidence (3-5 business days).
- **Additional Info Required** - the claim is paused until the customer supplies the outstanding items.
- **Approved** - settlement is being arranged.
- **Settled** - payment issued or replacement arranged; the claim closes automatically after 14 days.
- **Rejected** - see the rejection code; an appeal is available for 60 days.
- **Closed** - claim completed or withdrawn.

Agents can look up live status with the claim reference number (CLM-XXXXXXX). If a claim has been In Assessment for more than 5 business days, raise a follow-up with the claims team and tell the customer when to expect an update.
