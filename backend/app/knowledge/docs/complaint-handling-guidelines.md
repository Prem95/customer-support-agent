# Complaint Handling Guidelines
Keywords: complaint, unhappy, frustrated, angry, escalate, dissatisfied, poor service, feedback

When a customer expresses dissatisfaction:

1. **Acknowledge** the frustration explicitly before problem-solving. Do not argue or assign blame.
2. **Record** the complaint in the CRM with category (service, claim outcome, billing, product) — this is mandatory even if resolved immediately.
3. **Resolve or route**: agents may offer a goodwill excess-fee waiver (one per customer per year) for service failures. Claim-outcome disputes go through the appeal process, not goodwill gestures.
4. **Respond** within 24 hours with a written summary of the resolution or next steps.

Formal complaints (customer uses the word "complaint" or requests escalation) must receive a case number and a substantive response within 5 business days.
