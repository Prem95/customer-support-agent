# Policy Types and Cover Levels
Keywords: product, plan, policy type, cover level, price, premium, essential, standard, comprehensive, quote

Three cover levels are offered on personal policies:

| Cover level | Annual limit | Excess | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Essential | 5,000 | 250 | Accidental damage, fire, flood |
| Standard | 15,000 | 150 | Essential plus theft and loss |
| Comprehensive | 40,000 | 75 | Standard plus worldwide cover and liability |

All policies include:
- Unlimited claims within the annual limit
- 24-hour claims notification line
- 12-month term with monthly or annual payment

Premiums are quoted individually based on the declared items, cover level, and claims history. Items valued above the per-item limit must be declared separately at the time the policy is taken out or amended.
