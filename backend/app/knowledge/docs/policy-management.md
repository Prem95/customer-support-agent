# Policy Management
Keywords: policy, renew, renewal, cancel, cancellation, update, change, payment, premium, lapse, billing

Common policy operations:

- **Renewal** - policies renew automatically at the end of each term. Customers are notified 14 days before renewal with the new premium.
- **Cancellation** - customers may cancel at any time with 30 days notice. Refunds are pro-rated for annual policies; a cooling-off period of 14 days applies to new policies.
- **Payment failure** - a 14-day grace period follows a failed payment. Cover lapses if payment is not received within it, and incidents during a lapse are not covered.
- **Updating details** - contact and billing details can be changed at any time. Changes to insured items or cover levels require a policy amendment and may adjust the premium.
- **Cover changes** - increases take effect immediately; reductions take effect at the next renewal.

Policy numbers use the format POL-XXXXXXX. Agents must verify the policyholder's identity (full name plus registered email or the last four digits of the payment method) before making any change.
