# Claim Appeal Process
Keywords: appeal, dispute, review, rejected, overturn, reconsider

Customers may appeal a rejected claim within 60 days of the rejection notice.

Appeal steps:
1. Customer submits an appeal via the support portal, quoting the claim reference number and rejection code.
2. Customer attaches any new supporting evidence (photos, receipts, police report).
3. The appeals team reviews within 10 business days.
4. Outcome is sent by email; if upheld, the claim re-enters standard processing.

Agents cannot overturn rejections themselves but should help the customer understand the rejection code and what evidence would strengthen the appeal. One appeal is allowed per claim.
